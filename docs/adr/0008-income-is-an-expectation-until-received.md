# Income is an expectation until it is received

An Income schedule describes money expected on a cadence; it becomes real only when an occurrence is
received, at which point ledger credit entries are written to the wallets it was directed to. Booking
silently on the scheduled date would invent money that may not have arrived — a delayed or missed
salary would corrupt the balance and every forecast derived from it. Keeping income forecast-only would
make balances incomplete. The two-step model gives the user a correction path, which is the same
principle the rest of the app follows: record what happened, and let it be corrected.
